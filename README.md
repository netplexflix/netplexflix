## My current active Plex related stack

### Media Aquisition

* [Radarr](https://github.com/Radarr/Radarr): Automates acquisition of your legally owned movies
* [Sonarr](https://github.com/Sonarr/Sonarr): Same, but for TV shows
* [Prowlarr](https://github.com/Prowlarr/Prowlarr): Indexer manager for Radarr/Sonarr
* [Bazarr](https://github.com/morpheus65535/bazarr): Subtitle fetching & syncing
* [Ombi](https://github.com/Ombi-app/Ombi): User request system _(Alternatives: [Seerr](https://github.com/seerr-team/seerr), [Petio](https://github.com/petio-team/petio))_
* [Pulsarr](https://github.com/jamcalli/pulsarr): Real-time Plex watchlist monitoring and content acquisition tool.
* [MTDP](https://github.com/netplexflix/Missing-Trailer-Downloader-For-Plex) _(Missing Trailer Downloader for Plex)_: Downloads missing (Plex Pass) trailers
* [SABnzbd](https://sabnzbd.org/): Usenet downloader

### Monitoring & Management

* [Tautulli](https://github.com/Tautulli/Tautulli): Plex server monitoring & stats _(Alternative: [Tracearr](https://github.com/connorgallopo/tracearr))_
  * [Plex Wrapped for Tautulli](https://github.com/netplexflix/Plex-Wrapped-for-Tautulli): Beautiful Plex Wrapped reports based on Tautulli data 

### Metadata & Library Enhancements

* [Kometa](https://github.com/Kometa-Team/Kometa): Collections, overlays, and automation _(Alternative: [Agregarr](https://github.com/agregarr/agregarr))_
  * [UMTK](https://github.com/netplexflix/Upcoming-Movies-TV-Shows-for-Kometa) _(Upcoming Movies & TV Shows for Kometa)_:  
    - Create "Coming Soon" and "Trending" collections using a placeholder method. 
    - Creates overlay files for TV Show statuses.
* [UpdateTool](https://github.com/mynttt/UpdateTool): Updates IMDb ratings & sets IMDb badges
* [Remove from Plex Watchlist when watched](https://github.com/netplexflix/Remove-from-Plex-Watchlist-when-watched): Keeps watchlist clean

### Maintenance & Optimization

* [ULDAS](https://github.com/netplexflix/ULDAS) _(Unknown Language Detector for Audio and Subtitles)_: Detects & tags language of unknown/undefined audio and subtitle tracks
* [ImageMaid](https://github.com/Kometa-Team/ImageMaid): Cleans bloated Plex directories
* [MKV Optimizer](https://github.com/VideoProcessing/MKV-Optimizer): Removes unwanted tracks
* [jmkvpropedit](https://github.com/BrunoReX/jmkvpropedit): Fixes track metadata (e.g. language tags)

### Mobile & Client Apps
* [What to Watch on Plex](https://github.com/netplexflix/What-to-watch-on-Plex): Decide what to watch on your Plex server by setting preferences and swiping for group matches
* [QuasiTV](https://github.com/vexorian/quasitv): Creates “live TV channels” from your library
* [Tautulli Remote](https://tautulli.com/): Monitoring on the go
* [NZB360](https://nzb360.com/): Control Radarr/Sonarr/etc.
* [Plex Dash](https://www.plex.tv/plex-labs/): Quick poster edits & server stats
